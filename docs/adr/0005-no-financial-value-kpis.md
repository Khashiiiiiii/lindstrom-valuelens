# No Financial Value KPIs while the data holds no Customer cost

The only money field in the workbook is `synthetic_purchase_cost_eur` per product. Lindström rents the garments, so this is Lindström's cost, not the Customer's. Replacements postponed times purchase cost (€660,082 for NorthStar in 2025) and allocated garments with no scan activity at purchase cost (€214,371) would both present Lindström's cost as a Customer saving. The Customer would really save a rental fee, which the data does not hold. We define no Financial Value KPIs and no financial opportunity signals in this effort.

## Consequences

- The Financial Value evidence area stays in the catalogue and the exported review with one line: deferred, because the data holds no Customer cost or price.
- Replacements postponed and allocated garments with no scan activity stay counts. Replacements postponed feeds avoided product CO2e only.
- The "Cost" Customer priority maps to Garment utilisation and shows no euro figure.
- No Customer emissions figure per euro exists. The group intensity of 0.28 kg CO2e per euro is a public context line and is never multiplied into a Customer figure.
