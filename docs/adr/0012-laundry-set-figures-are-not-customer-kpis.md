# Figures the laundry sets are not Customer KPIs

Water, energy, CO2e and textile waste reach a Customer only through allocation by washed-kg share. Per washed kg, these figures are laundry factors: waste per washed kg is fixed per laundry for all 12 months, and process CO2e per kWh runs from 0.05 to 0.42 with the country grid. The Customer has no lever on them. We decided that footprint per washed kg and textile waste per washed kg are not Customer KPIs. They appear only in the KAM version of Data transparency, where they explain why Customers differ.

## Consequences

- Footprint KPIs offer a total or a per-active-wearer unit, never per washed kg.
- Account health, opportunity signals and Improvement comparisons read no footprint, handprint or waste route figure.
- Caveats on allocated figures say they are the Customer's share of laundry totals, not measured at the Site.

Source: [Footprint and handprint KPIs JSON](https://github.com/Khashiiiiiii/lindstrom-valuelens/issues/5), [Operational and circularity KPIs JSON](https://github.com/Khashiiiiiii/lindstrom-valuelens/issues/4).
