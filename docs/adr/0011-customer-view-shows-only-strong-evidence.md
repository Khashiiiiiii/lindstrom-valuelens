# The Customer view and the exported review leave out weak evidence

The KAM view and the Customer view share one first screen. The Customer view, and the exported review that follows its rules, leaves out results that are weak evidence or rest on invented thresholds: account health, opportunity signal 4 (service request rate, which mostly flags small Sites), signal results that did not fire or were not evaluated, Improvement results at Site grain, and the full Data transparency detail. The Customer view gets its own Data transparency version with the baseline, assumption labels, data status legend and data gaps. We chose this so that every figure a Customer decision-maker sees can be defended in the meeting, while the KAM still sees everything.

## Consequences

- Every catalogue entry records a `view`. The rule applies to LLM answers too: in the Customer view the LLM cannot return a KAM-only result. Enforcement is app work.
- In 2025 the Customer view shows 13 fired signal results for NorthStar, against 18 in the KAM view.
- The exported review has 5 parts (Look back, Show value, Build trust, Look forward, Agree actions), with 65% evidence and value against 35% future planning as a page-count target.
